Fairness is the foundation of lasting peace between peoples.

Peace without justice is fragile. Noble Lions believes that respect for civil rights, rule of law, and human dignity creates the conditions in which cultures can meet as equals and build lasting bonds.

    Social Reintegration Supporting individuals to rejoin society and contributing to community healing strengthens every culture from within.

How to Develop Self-Reliance

Practical steps — each one a building block on the path to independent, purposeful living.

01 Root yourself in faith & spiritual clarity ›

Begin every day with prayer, reflection, or meditation. Let spiritual grounding be the compass that shapes your choices. When your foundation is the Creator, you gain a clarity that cannot be shaken by circumstances.

ነፍሲ ወከፍ መዓልቲ ኣብ ጸሎትን ምሕሳብን ጀምር ። ፈጣሪ ናይ ሂወትካ ኣቅጣጫ ኣለዎ — ናቱ ናቅ ፍቐዶ ።

02 Build daily habits of self-discipline ›

Set clear goals and track your progress. Wake with intention, work with focus, rest with purpose. Small daily disciplines — practised consistently — compound into profound personal freedom and independence.

ዕላማ ኣውጽእ፡ ናይ ዕለት ዕለት ስርዓት ምዕባዩ ። ኣሽቱ ልምድታት ዝዓበዩ ናጽነት ዘምጽኡ ።

03 Commit to lifelong learning ›

Read, study, ask questions, and seek mentors. Knowledge is the currency of self-reliance — the more you learn, the more doors open to you. Never stop growing your mind and your skills.

ኣብ ምምሃር ኣይደው ። ፍልጠት ናይ ርእሰ ምርኮሳ ዋጋ እዩ — ዝበዝሐ ዝተምሃርካ፡ ዝዛሓ ናጻ ።

04 Develop financial awareness & responsibility ›

Understand how money works, learn to save consistently, avoid unnecessary debt, and build resources step by step. Financial literacy is one of the most practical expressions of self-reliance.

ናይ ገንዘብ ፍልጠት ኣምዕብሉ — ምቁጣብ፡ ዕዳ ምርካብ ምምሕያሽ፡ ሃብቲ ምህናጽ ። ናይ ኢኮኖሚ ናጽነት ናይ ርእሰ ምርኮሳ ሓደ ቀንዲ መለክዒ እዩ ።

05 Cultivate relationships that grow you ›

Choose friends, mentors and community who challenge you to grow, support you when you fall, and celebrate your progress. No one becomes self-reliant alone — the right people accelerate your journey.

ዘዕቢኡ ዝምድናታት ምምዕባሉ — ዝሕግዙ፡ ዝደፋፍኡ፡ ናብ ዓቢ ሰብ ዝቕይሩ ።

06 Take full responsibility for your choices ›

Own your decisions and their consequences — both successes and failures. Stop attributing everything to circumstances and start responding to them. Responsibility is the heart of self-reliance.

ናይ ርእስኻ ምርጫታትን ውጺኢቶምን ሓላፍነት ወስድ ። ናትካ ሕይወት ብናትካ ኢድ ።

07 Give back — serve your community ›

True self-reliance does not end with you — it overflows into service to others. When you help lift another person, your own ground grows firmer. The highest expression of independence is choosing to give.

ናይ ርእሰ ምርኮሳ ፍሬ — ካልኦት ምሕጋዝ ። ካልኦት ኣብ ርእሶም ምቃም — ናትካ ሰረት ዘደልዳሉ ።

What is Governance?

Understanding the principles and practice of governing ourselves, our communities, and our institutions.

ስነ ምሕደራ — The Meaning of Governance

Governance is the system of rules, practices, processes and values by which a community, organisation, or nation directs itself — making decisions, distributing power, and fulfilling responsibilities to those it serves.

Good governance is not just about who holds authority — it is about how that authority is exercised: with transparency, accountability, fairness, and in the interests of all people.

ስነ ምሕደራ ማለት — ሕብረተሰብ፡ ትካልን ሃገርን ብስርዓት፡ ብሕጊ፡ ብሓቀኛ ናይ ሓቢርካ ኣሰራርሓን ዝምዳን ዝካየደሉ ኣሰራርሓ እዩ ። ናይ ርእሰ ምሕደራ ዋና ሕቶ — ስልጣን ብኸመይ ይካየድ?

How is Self-Governance Conducted?

Six essential mechanisms — from principles to practice — that enable genuine self-governance at every level: personal, community, and national.

01 Establish clear principles & shared values ›

Every system of self-governance begins with agreed values — honesty, dignity, fairness, and service. These principles act as the constitution of behaviour that binds leaders and citizens alike, providing a compass for every decision made in the collective interest.

ርእሰ ምሕደራ ካብ ዝተሰማምዑሉ ክብርታት ይጅምር — ሓቅነት፡ ክብርን፡ ፍትሕን ። እዚ ክብርታት ናይ ኩሉ ምሕደራ ቅዋም እዩ ።

02 Build transparent structures & clear roles ›

Good governance requires clear organisational structures — defined roles, responsibilities, and decision-making processes that everyone understands. When people know who is responsible for what and how decisions are made, trust is built and confusion is avoided.

ሓደ ዝፈለጡዎ ዕማምን ሓላፍነትን ዘለዎም ትካላዊ ቅርጺ — ናይ ምሕደራ ሰረት ። ኩሉ ሰብ ናቱ ዕማም ዝፈልጠሉ ስርዓት ምምጻእ ።

03 Ensure accountability & oversight ›

Leaders and institutions must be answerable for their decisions and actions. Accountability systems — audits, reviews, reporting, and consequences for misconduct — are the guardrails that prevent abuse of power and keep governance honest and effective.

ሓለፍቲ ናይ ዝወስዶዎ ውሳነ ሓላፍነት ዝወስዱሉ ስርዓት ምምጻእ — ርእሰ ምሕደራ ካብ ዕሱብ ዝሕሉ ።

04 Enable broad participation & inclusion ›

Self-governance thrives when every voice has a place in decision-making. Inclusive processes — consultations, elections, open forums, and community dialogue — ensure that governance reflects the real needs and aspirations of all people, not just a privileged few.

ናይ ኩሎም ድምጺ ዝሰምዕ ምሕደራ — ናይ ህዝቢ ምሕደራ እዩ ። ምርጫ፡ ምምኻር፡ ቁሊሕ ምዝራብ — ናይ ርእሰ ምሕደራ ኣካላት ።

05 Uphold the rule of law equally ›

Laws must apply equally to all people — the powerful and the powerless alike. The rule of law creates a level playing field, protects individual rights, and prevents governance from becoming the instrument of personal interest. Without it, self-governance collapses into domination.

ሕጊ ንኩሉ ሰብ ማዕረ ዝምልከት ክኸውን ኣለዎ — ሓይለኛ ኮነ ድኹም ። ልዕልና ሕጊ — ናይ ርእሰ ምሕደራ ዓምዲ ።

06 Commit to continuous learning & improvement ›

No governance system is perfect from the start. The mark of good self-governance is its willingness to evaluate, learn from mistakes, and reform. Regular reviews, feedback loops, and the courage to change course are what transform adequate governance into excellent governance.

ርእሰ ምሕደራ ኣይውዳእን — ዘሻሻሉ፡ ካብ ጌጋ ዝምሃሩ፡ ዝዓቢ ስርዓት ዝምህ ። ቀጻሊ ምምሕያሽ ናይ ሓቀኛ ምሕደራ ምልክት ።

Different Types of Governance

Governance takes many forms across societies — each shaped and evaluated through the lens of that society's culture, values, and beliefs.

💰
ናይ ፋይናንሳዊ ምሕደራ

Financial Governance

Financial governance is the system of rules, processes, and structures through which resources — money, assets, and economic opportunities — are managed, allocated, and accounted for within an organisation, community, or nation.

  • Budgeting & fiscal responsibility
  • Transparency in spending & auditing
  • Equitable distribution of resources
  • Anti-corruption measures & oversight
  • Long-term economic planning
Cultural dimension:

Every culture holds values around wealth, generosity, and equity. Financial governance that reflects a society's ethic of fairness and collective responsibility earns trust and sustains prosperity across generations.

ናይ ፋይናንሳዊ ምሕደራ ጥቕሚ — ሃብቲ ብፍትሕን ብንጽህናን ምምሕዳሩ ። ሕብረተሰብ ብናቱ ናይ ምምቅቃል ክብርታት ዝምዝኖ ።

🏛️
ህዝባዊ ምሕደራ

Public & Administrative Governance

Public governance is the system through which governments, institutions, and civil authorities organise and deliver services, uphold rights, and manage collective affairs in the interest of all citizens — built on law, representation, and accountability.

  • Democratic elections & representation
  • Rule of law & constitutional order
  • Delivery of public services
  • Civil rights & protection of minorities

  • Dispute resolution through faith traditions
  • Stewardship of community and charitable giving
  • Spiritual accountability before the Creator

Each type of governance is shaped by culture and belief. No single model fits every society. The most effective governance — financial, public, or religious — grows from the authentic values of the people it serves, earning legitimacy not by imposition but by alignment with what that community holds most sacred and true.

What This Declaration Means

This ancient Ge'ez phrase is a declaration of faithful witness. To say "ማእምራን እምንቱ" — "we are faithful believers" — is not merely a statement of religious belonging. It is a covenant: a commitment to be trustworthy in word, deed, and character.

The word "ንቤለክሙ" — "we say to you" — gives this declaration a communal, outward direction. It is a witness made before others: we do not simply hold this truth privately — we declare it, live it, and invite others to hold us accountable to it.

ማእምራን እምንቱ — ናብ ቃልና፡ ናብ ተግባርናን ናብ ባህርይናን እምነታዊ ምዃን ። ምስክርነት ኣብ ቅድሚ ካልኦት — ናብ ሓቂ ናይ ምቁማት ቃልኪዳን ።

I
It is the source of all action

No person acts without some belief that action is worth taking. Hope is the engine beneath every human effort — remove it and all motion stops. Sustaining hope is sustaining the capacity to change your situation.

It protects community

When one person loses hope, those around them feel it. When a community collectively loses hope, it fragments. A society that nurtures hope in its members — especially in the young — is a society that will endure and grow.

III
It turns pain into purpose

Hardship without hope is pure suffering. Hardship held within hope becomes a story — a test, a chapter, a force that shapes character. Hope gives difficulty a frame: this is not the end; this is the making of something better.

ጭንቀት ብተስፋ ዝሕዞ — ዛንታ ይኸውን፡ ፈተና፡ ባህርይ ዝቐርጽ ።

IV
It is a duty to those who come after

Those who keep hope alive do not do so only for themselves. Every elder who models perseverance, every parent who refuses to despair, every young person who rises again after falling — they are keeping alive a fire the next generation will need.

ተስፋ ዝሕልዉ ንርእሶም ጥራሕ ኣይኮኑን — ናብ ዝቕጽሉ ወለዶ ጋዜ ዝሕልዉ ።

At every moment — never surrender, never lose hope. This is our choice and our strength.

😊
It creates joy and happiness

Growing together produces a feeling that individual achievement rarely can — the shared delight of watching people around you rise alongside you. Joy multiplies when it is witnessed and celebrated in community.

ሓቢርካ ምዕባይ ዘይምዕብኡ ደስታ ይፈጥር — ኣብ ሕብረተሰብ ዝረጋገጽ ሓጎስ ።

⚡
Its results appear swiftly

When people combine their skills, energy, and resources, the pace of progress accelerates dramatically. What one person might achieve in years, a committed collective can accomplish in months.

ናይ ሓባር ጻዕሪ ፍጥነቱ ዝሓይሽ ውጽኢት ብቕልጡፍ ይፈጥር ።

🌱
It builds lasting strength

A community that grows together builds roots — shared history, mutual trust, and structures that survive beyond any single individual. This is the kind of strength that one generation passes to the next.

ሓቢርካ ዝዓበኻ ሕብረተሰብ ናብ ዝቕጽሉ ወለዶ ዝሓልፍ ሓይሊ ይሃንጽ ።

እንታይ ፍረ · What Results

The Visible Fruits of Collective Growth

Stronger bonds

ሓቢርካ ዝሰርሕ ህዝቢ ዝሓሸ እምነት ይህሉ ። ምትሕብባር ናይ ሓፈሻ ልምዲ ይኸውን ።

Visible, shared prosperity

Resources and opportunities spread more widely. Prosperity is not concentrated in the hands of a few — it becomes visible across the whole community.

ሃብትን ዕድልን ናብ ሕብረተሰብ ኩሉ ክፋሉ ይዝርጋሕ — ናብ ሒደት ኣይጸምቀን ።

🎯

Shared purpose and direction

Growing together aligns people around common goals. This shared purpose gives meaning to daily effort and removes the isolation of working alone.

ሓቢርካ ምዕባይ ህዝቢ ኣብ ሓደ ዕላማ የሰልፍ — ዕለታዊ ጻዕሪ ትርጉም ይህቦ ።

🌍

Resilience under pressure

Communities that have grown together withstand shocks — economic downturns, hardship, external pressures — far better than isolated individuals.

ሓቢርካ ዝዓበኻ ሕብረተሰብ ናይ ድሑር ኩነታት ዓቕሚ ዝሓሸ ።

ከመይ ንተግብሮ · How To Practise

Practical Steps Toward Collective Growth

Collective growth does not happen automatically — it is built step by step, through deliberate choices and sustained habits.

1 Shift from "I" to "we"

The first and most fundamental step is a change in frame: begin every plan, every project, and every decision by asking not "what do I gain?" but "what do we build?" This mental shift changes the energy of everything that follows.

ናይ ሓሳብ ለውጢ — "ኣነ" ካብ "ንሕና" ። ዝኾነ መደብ ናብ "እንታይ ንሃንጽ" ብምቕያር ።

2 Invest in others' capacity

Active collective growth requires deliberately helping others rise. Share knowledge, open doors, mentor those behind you, and celebrate others' progress publicly. What you invest in others returns multiplied.

ካልኦት ናብ ዝዓቢ ምሕጋዝ — ፍልጠት ምክፋል፡ ናይ ካልኦት ዕወት ምቅላስ ።

3 Build shared projects and spaces

Create tangible things together — a community initiative, a shared resource, a collective goal with milestones. Shared projects are the engine of shared identity. They give abstract values a concrete form people can see and touch.

ሓቢርካ ዝርኣ ነገራት ምፍጣር — ናይ ሕብረተሰብ ተበግሶ፡ ሓቢርካ ዝቕጽሉ ዕላማ ።

4 Celebrate collective wins visibly

Every collective milestone deserves public recognition. Celebrating together reinforces that growth is shared, not individual. It signals to newcomers and doubters alike that this community moves — and that they are welcome to move with it.

ናይ ሓባር ዕወት ብሓደ ምቅላስ — ሕብረተሰብ ዝዓቢ ምዃኑ ናብ ኩሉ ምርኣይ ።

🌿

ሓቢርና ንዓቢ — ብዙሕ ደስታን ሓጎስን ይፈጥሩ ፣ ብቑልጡፍ ዝርኤ ውጽኢት ።

Together we grow — creating abundant joy, happiness, and results that appear swiftly for all to see.

When We Achieve Victory, We Deserve to Rest

Victory is not only a moment of triumph — it is the right to finally rest from the exhausting cycle of life's ups and downs. We have earned it. We must honour it.

ዓወት ምስ ተጎናጸፍና —

ሂወትና ካብ ላዕልን ታሕን ዘብል ድኻም ከነዕርፋ ።

When we have achieved victory — we should let our lives rest from the exhausting cycle of ups and downs.

I
ዓወት · What Victory Means

What True Victory Looks Like

Victory is not merely a single achievement — it is the culmination of sustained effort, sacrifice, and perseverance. Understanding what we have won changes how we receive the rest we deserve.

🏆

The end of a long struggle

True victory marks the close of a chapter filled with hardship. It is the moment when what once felt impossible has been made real through patience and relentless effort.

ዓወት ናይ ነዊሕ ቃልሲ መፈጸምታ — ዘይኽኣለ ዝነበረ ብጽንዓት ዝተረኽበ ።

⚖️

Balance restored

The constant oscillation between highs and lows is exhausting. Victory restores equilibrium — a steady ground to stand on, free from the anxiety of the next fall.

ዓወት ናይ ሂወት ሚዛን ዘምለሰ — ካብ ናይ ድኻም ላዕልን ታሕን ናጻ ዝኾነ ።

🌅

A new beginning, freely chosen

Once you have won, the next chapter belongs entirely to you — not driven by necessity or fear, but by choice, by joy, and by a life lived on your own terms.

ዓወት ዝስዕቦ ምዕራፍ ናትካ — ብምርጫን ሓጎስን ዝምራሕ ሂወት ።

II
ንምንታይ · Why Rest Matters

Why We Must Rest after Victory

Rest is not weakness and it is not surrender — it is the intelligent response to achievement. Here is why rest is essential after victory.

🧘
The body and mind need recovery

The energy spent to achieve victory is real. Every struggle consumes physical and mental reserves. Rest is not idleness — it is the repair process that makes the next chapter possible. Without it, we carry yesterday's exhaustion into tomorrow.

ሂወትካ ዓወት ምስ ዘምጽእ ዝተጠቀምካዮ ሓይሊ ከነብሰ ናብ ምሕዳሱ ዝጸውዕ — ዕረፍቲ ዝቕጽል ምዕራፍ ዝሃንጽ ።

🎯
Clarity returns with stillness

When life is an endless cycle of struggle, we lose the ability to see clearly. Rest creates the stillness in which vision returns — where we can ask what we truly want next, not just what the crisis demands.

ዕረፍቲ ንጹር ሓሳብ ዘምጽእ — ካብ ቅልውላው ዘይኮነስ ካብ ምርጫ ዝሕቶ ዝቕጽሎ ።

🌿
It honours the victory itself

Rushing immediately into the next battle dishonours what was just won. Pausing to rest is an act of gratitude — a conscious recognition that something real and meaningful has been achieved. It closes the chapter properly.

ዕረፍቲ ዝተረኽበ ዓወት ምኽባር — ዝተዓወትካዮ ዘክሩ ዝዓጽዎ ።

III
ከመይ ዕረፍቲ · How to Rest Well

Practical Steps toward Meaningful Rest

Rest is a practice — it does not always come naturally after a long season of striving. These steps make it real and sustainable.

1 Name and receive the victory

Before rest can begin, the victory must be acknowledged — said aloud, shared with others, and truly received. Many people rush past their wins without pausing to take them in. Name what you have achieved. Let it land. Only then can rest begin from a place of genuine completion.

ቅድሚ ዕረፍቲ ዓወት ብሓቂ ምቕባሉ — ምዝርራቡ፡ ካልኦት ምኽፋሉ፡ ምቕባሉ ። ሽዑ ጥራሕ ዕረፍቲ ካብ ዝተወደአ ምዕራፍ ይጅምር ።

2 Create space free from striving

Rest requires that striving be consciously suspended — not forever, but for a defined season. Create literal space: quiet time, a slower pace, activities that restore rather than demand. Protect this space against the habit of constant productivity. Not every moment needs to be earned.

ዕረፍቲ ካብ ቀጻሊ ጸዕሪ ሓርነት ዝሓትት — ዝጥዕዕ ምቕናስ ። ናይ ጸጥታ ጊዜ፡ ዝቕምርር ናብርኻ ። ነዚ ቦታ ካብ ናይ ቀጻሊ ምምራሕ ልምዲ ምሕላዉ ።

3 Reconnect with what brings simple joy

During seasons of struggle, simple pleasures are often postponed. Rest is the moment to return to them — relationships, creativity, nature, laughter, food shared with people you love. These are not rewards for the future. They are the substance of a life well lived, available now.

ሂወትካ ኣብ ቃልሲ ዝሃቀፍካዮ ዝቐናጸፈ ሓጎሳት ናብኦም ምምላስ — ዝምድናታት፡ ፍጠረ-ዓቐን፡ ናይ ስድርኦ ሓጎሳት ። ሕጂ ኣለዉ ።

4 Let the next chapter emerge, don't force it

True rest means releasing the compulsion to immediately plan the next conquest. Allow yourself the unscheduled time to simply be. The next vision will emerge with far greater clarity and depth from a rested mind than from an exhausted one forcing answers. Trust the process of stillness.

ቀጻሊ ምዕራፍ ናብ ዝቐጽሎ ዝቐናጸፈ — ዘይተሸጠ ጊዜ ምሃብ ። ዝቕጽሉ ሓሳባት ካብ ዝዕረፈ ሓሳብ ዝናሑ — ዝደኸመ ሓሳብ ዘይናሑ ።

🌄

ዓወት ምስ ተጎናጸፍና — ሂወትና ካብ ድኻም ዕረፍቲ ትረክብ ። ንዕረፍቲ ሕጂ ይወሃቦ ።

When victory is won — our lives deserve to rest from the exhaustion of the struggle. That rest begins now.

We Must Turn the World's Conditions to Our Benefit

The conditions that exist across the entire world are not our enemies — they are our raw material. With strategy and wisdom, we can channel them to serve our advancement, our community, and our future.

ናብ ጥቅምና ከነውዕሎ ክንክእል ኣለና ብሜላን ብጥበብን ።

The existing conditions across the entire world — we must be able to turn them to our benefit, with strategy and wisdom.

I
ህላው ኩነታት · Reading the World

What World Conditions Actually Are

Before we can turn conditions to our benefit, we must see them clearly. The world's conditions are not fixed obstacles — they are dynamic forces that can be understood, navigated, and redirected by those willing to look carefully.

🌐

Global shifts create openings

Every era of global change — economic, technological, political, cultural — creates new openings for those prepared to act. What appears as disruption to the unprepared is opportunity to the strategically aware.

ናይ ዓለም ለውጢ ንዕቡያት ዕድሚ ዝፈጥር — ዘይተዳልወ ዘሸበሮ ንዕቡይ ዕድሎ ።

📡

Information is the new leverage

In today's world, the ability to read patterns — in markets, in politics, in technology, in culture — is itself power. Those who understand the landscape before others act early and act with precision.

ናይ ዓለም ቅርጺ ምንባብ ሓይሊ ። ቀዲሙ ዝርዳእ ቀዲሙ ብዕምቆት ይሰርሕ ።

⚙️

Conditions respond to intentional action

Conditions are not passive. They respond to pressure, to organised effort, to sustained presence. Communities that act with collective intention do not merely adapt to the world — they reshape their corner of it.

ኩነታት ናብ ዕቁብ ጻዕሪ ዝምለሱ — ሓቢሩ ዝሰርሕ ማሕበረሰብ ዓለሙ ዝቕይር ።

II
ብሜላን ብጥበብን · Strategy & Wisdom

Why Strategy and Wisdom Are the Keys

Raw effort alone cannot turn global conditions to our advantage. Strategy tells us where to direct energy; wisdom tells us when, how, and for whom. Together they transform reactive survival into intentional advancement.

🧭
Strategy converts effort into direction

Without strategy, even great effort scatters. Strategy is the art of choosing which conditions to engage, which to ignore, and how to position for maximum return. It is the difference between pushing against a wall and walking through an open door.

ሜላ ጻዕሪ ናብ ሓቀኛ ቦታ ዘቕርብ — ዝሕሉ ዘይኮነስ ዝኸፈት ማዕጾ ዝርኢ ።

💡
Wisdom prevents costly mistakes

The world's conditions include traps as well as opportunities. Wisdom — built from experience, counsel, and honest reflection — is what allows us to move fast without stumbling into avoidable disasters. Wisdom is strategy tempered by humility.

ጥበብ ካብ ዝደናጎሉ ጌጋታት ዝሕሉ — ዕቁብ ምምልማልን ትሑት ኣወዳደርን ።

🤝
Together, they multiply impact

Strategy without wisdom becomes manipulation. Wisdom without strategy becomes passive. Together — applied by a united community — they create leverage that no individual effort alone can generate. This is why collective strategic wisdom is our greatest asset.

ሜላን ጥበብን ሓቢሮም ናይ ኩሉ ዘይዕርፍ ምልዓል — ናይ ሓቢርና ዝለዓለ ሓይሊ ።

III
ከናውዕሎ · Turning Conditions into Benefit

How to Turn World Conditions to Our Advantage

Awareness and intention are the start — but action is what converts understanding into real benefit. These four steps make strategic advantage practical and replicable.

1 Map the conditions around you

The first move is honest mapping — studying what is actually happening in the economic, political, social, and technological environment you inhabit. Do not rely on second-hand summaries. Seek primary sources, diverse perspectives, and your own analysis. Understanding comes before action; misreading the conditions is the most common source of strategic failure.

ቀዳማይ ስጕምቲ ብቐጥታ ኩነታት ምጽናዕ — ቁጠባዊ፡ ፖለቲካዊ፡ ማሕበራዊ፡ ቴክኖሎጂ ። ናይ ቀዳሞት ምንጭታት ። ምስ ምርዳእ ጥራሕ ስጕምቲ ።

2 Identify the openings that serve your purpose

Not every condition is relevant to your advancement. Strategic wisdom involves selection — identifying which openings align with your community's strengths, values, and goals. The art is not to chase everything, but to move decisively toward the conditions that genuinely serve your purpose. Focused energy outperforms scattered energy every time.

ኩሉ ኩነታት ናትካ ዘይኮነ ። ናትካን ናይ ማሕበረሰብካን ሓይልን ዕድምን ዝሰማምዑ ዕድላት ምፍላይ ። ዝተዓቀነ ሓይሊ ኩሉ ጊዜ ይሕሸ ።

3 Build the capacity to act before the window closes

Conditions shift. The opening that exists today may not exist tomorrow. Strategic advantage therefore requires that communities build capacity in advance — skills, networks, resources, and trust — so that when the right condition arises, they can act immediately and with force. Preparation is the bridge between awareness and result.

ኩነታት ይቕየሩ ። ዕድሊ ናይ ሎሚ ናይ ጽባሕ ኣይኮነን ። ቅሩብ ምዃን — ክእለት፡ ዝምድናታት፡ ሓይሊ — ቅድሚ ኹነታት ምምጽኡ ። ምዳሎ ናይ ምርዳእ ናብ ውጽኢት ድልድል ።

4 Act collectively, review continuously

Individual action is limited. Collective strategic action — coordinated across a community, aligned on purpose, and continuously reviewed — is what creates durable change. After each move, review what worked and what did not. Adjust the strategy. The world does not stay still, and neither should we. Continuous learning is what separates those who capture lasting advantage from those who capture it once and lose it.

ናይ ውልቀ ጻዕሪ ዕድሙ ውሱን ። ናይ ሓቢርና ስትራቴጂካዊ ምቅስቃስ — ዝቐጸለ ፍተሻን ምምሕያሽን — ዘለዓለም ረብሓ ዝፈጥር ።

🌍

ህላው ኩነታት ኣብ ኩሉ ዓለም ናብ ጥቅምና ዘውዕሎ — ብሜላን ብጥበብን ።

We have the capacity to turn the world's conditions to our benefit — with strategy, with wisdom, and together.

Growth with Wisdom Does Not Make Life Easier — It Makes Life Stronger

Growing with wisdom and emotional intelligence is not the path to a simpler life. It is the path to a life that rises to meet you — because as your knowledge, wisdom, and discernment deepen, life itself grows stronger in proportion to your growth.

ሂወት ቅልል ይብል ማለት ኣይኮነን ።

ምኽንያቱ ብፍልጠትን ጥበብን ምስትውዓልን ኣብ እንዓብየሉ እዋን —

ሂወት ማዕረ ዕብየትና እና ጠንከረ ይከይድ ።

If we can grow with wisdom and emotional intelligence — it does not mean life becomes easier. Because when we grow with knowledge, wisdom, and discernment — life grows stronger in direct proportion to our growth.

I
ናይ ዕቤት ሓቂ · The Truth about Growth

What Growing with Wisdom Actually Means

Most people pursue growth hoping life will become lighter. The truth is more demanding — and more rewarding. Growth with wisdom does not reduce the weight of life; it builds the strength to carry greater weight with greater grace.

⚖️

Life scales with your capacity

A person who has grown in wisdom does not encounter smaller challenges — they encounter challenges equal to their expanded capacity. The mountain grows with the climber. This is not a burden; it is the proof that growth has been real.

ዝዓበኻ ሰብ ናእሽቱ ብድሆ ኣይረክብን — ምስ ዝዓበኻ ዓቕምኻ ዝዛመዱ ብድሆ ። ደብሪ ምስ ደናግዱ ዕቤት — ዕቤትካ ሓቀኛ ምዃኑ ምርጋጽ ።

🧠

Wisdom reveals complexity, not simplicity

Ignorance feels simple because it cannot see what is actually there. Wisdom sees clearly — and clarity reveals layers. The wise person understands more, feels more, and must navigate more. This is the cost and the gift of knowing.

ድንቁርና ቀሊሉ ስለ ዘይርኢ — ጥበብ ብንጹር ዝርኢ ። ዝፈለጠ ሰብ ዝያዳ ዝርኢ፡ ዝያዳ ዝሓስብ ። ናይ ምፍላጥ ዋጋን ህያብን ።

🌱

Emotional intelligence deepens, not removes, feeling

Emotional intelligence does not make you numb to life's hardships. It makes you capable of experiencing them more fully — and processing them more deeply. The emotionally intelligent person does not feel less; they feel with greater precision and channel that feeling into purposeful action.

ስነልቦና ዓቕሚ ዝሽቃቐቐካ ዝገብር ኣይኮነን — ብዕምቆት ምስዓሩ ዝሕግዝ ። ስነልቦናዊ ዕቡይ ንኡሽቱ ዘይስምዖ — ብዕምቆትን ዕላማን ።

II
ሂወት ምስ ዕቤትካ ዝጠንከር · Why Life Strengthens

Why Life Grows Stronger as You Grow

The strengthening of life in proportion to your growth is not a punishment — it is the architecture of meaningful existence. Here is why this is not only inevitable, but necessary.

🏗️
Greater capacity attracts greater responsibility

When you grow in wisdom and knowledge, the world — your community, your family, your peers — begins to bring you its more complex problems. This is not unfair; it is the natural consequence of becoming capable. Responsibility is the currency of true growth, and it compounds as you do.

ዝዓቀነ ዓቕሚ ዝዓቀነ ሓላፍነት ዘምጽእ ። ዝፈለጠ ሰብ ዓለሙ ዝዓቀነ ሕቶ ዘቕርበሉ — ቅኑዕ ። ሓላፍነት ናይ ሓቀኛ ዕቤት ዋጋ ።

🔥
Easy lives do not produce wisdom

Wisdom is forged in difficulty, not in comfort. If life became easier as you grew, the very conditions that produce wisdom would disappear. The strengthening of life's challenges is what ensures wisdom continues to be refined — always one step ahead of where it was.

ጥበብ ካብ ቃልሲ ዝሕቀቕ — ካብ ምቾት ዘይኮነ ። ሂወት ምስ ዓበኻ ቀሊሉ እንተ ዝኸውን — ጥበብ ዝፈጥሩ ኩነታት ምጠፍኡ ።

🎯
Stronger challenges reveal deeper purpose

The challenges that come with real growth are not random — they are the specific tests that reveal what you are truly for. They surface your deepest values, your highest capabilities, and your most meaningful contribution. A life that strengthens with you is a life that is becoming what it was meant to be.

ናይ ሓቀኛ ዕቤት ብድሆ ዘይሰካሕ — ናትካ ዕላማ ዘርኢ ። ዝዓቀነ ብድሆ ዝዓቀነ ዕላማ ।

III
ከምይ ብጥበብ ምዕባይ · Growing Wisely

How to Grow with Knowledge, Wisdom, and Discernment

Knowing that life strengthens with you does not make it automatic. Growth must be intentional — pursued through deliberate practice, honest self-examination, and a willingness to be changed by what you learn.

1 Pursue knowledge as a daily discipline

Knowledge is not a destination — it is a practice. Those who grow with wisdom treat learning not as an occasional event but as a daily discipline: reading, observing, asking questions, seeking out people who know more, and reflecting on what experience has taught. The gap between those who grow and those who stay still is almost always a gap in the discipline of learning.

ፍልጠት መዕረፍ ዘለዎ ኣይኮነን — ቀዋሚ ልምዲ ። ዝዓቀነ ዕቤት ዘለዎ ሰብ ምምሃር ናቱ ዕለታዊ ተግባር — ምንባብ፡ ምስትውዓል፡ ምሕታት፡ ካብ ዝፈለጡ ምምሃር ።

2 Develop emotional intelligence through honest reflection

Emotional intelligence grows through honest self-examination — not flattery, not avoidance, but clear and compassionate inquiry into your own reactions, patterns, and blind spots. Ask: why did I respond that way? What was I afraid of? What did that situation reveal about me? This kind of honest inner work, done regularly, is what separates those who grow emotionally from those who simply grow older.

ስነልቦና ብሓቀኛ ምምርማር ናይ ርእስኻ ዝዓቢ — ምምሃዝ ዘይኮነ ። ስምዒትካ ዘጎደሎ፡ ዘጎሃረካ ቅርጺ ምፍላጥ ። ቀዋሚ ናይ ውሽጢ ምምርማር ።

3 Welcome difficulty as the teacher it is

Because life strengthens in proportion to your growth, the difficult seasons are not signs that something has gone wrong — they are signs that something is going right. When life becomes harder, ask: what is this teaching me? What capacity is being built in me through this? Reframing difficulty as curriculum, rather than punishment, is one of the most powerful shifts a growing person can make.

ሂወት ምስ ዓበኻ ዝጠንከር ስለዝኾነ — ናይ ቃልሲ ዘመን ጌጋ ምልክት ኣይኮነን — ቅኑዕ ምልክት ። ቃልሲ ናይ ዕቤት ትምህርቲ ። ዘሸበሮ ዘይኮነ ዝምህር ።

4 Let discernment guide action, not just impulse

Discernment — ምስትውዓል — is the capacity to distinguish what is truly important from what merely feels urgent, what is genuinely right from what is merely comfortable, what serves your long-term purpose from what gratifies the moment. Practising discernment means pausing before acting, weighing consequences, and choosing deliberately. It is the bridge between knowledge and wisdom — and between wisdom and a life that is truly well lived.

ምስትውዓል — ኣገዳሲ ካብ ዝጎሃሮ፡ ሓቀኛ ካብ ዝምቸሎ፡ ናይ ነዊሕ ዕላማ ካብ ናይ ህሞት ምፍላይ ። ቅድሚ ስጕምቲ ምምዛን — ናይ ፍልጠትን ጥበብን ናይ ዝዓቀነ ሂወት ድልድል ።

🌿

ብፍልጠትን ጥበብን ምስትውዓልን ምዕባይ ሂወት ዝቐልሎ ኣይኮነ — ሂወት ምስና ዝጠንከር ። ሂወት ምስ ዕቤትና ።

Growing with knowledge, wisdom, and discernment does not make life easier — it makes life stronger alongside us. And that is exactly as it should be.

ታሪኽ ምስራሕ · Making History

Our Existence in the World — We Must Be Able to Make History

Existence is not enough. Our presence in this world carries a responsibility greater than survival — we must leave a mark that future generations can point to and say: they were here, and the world was changed because of it.

ህላዌና ኣብ ዓለም —

ታሪኽ ክንሰርሕ ክንክእል ኣለና ።

Our existence in the world — we must be able to make history.

I
ታሪኽ ምስራሕ · What It Means

What Making History Actually Looks Like

Making history is not reserved for kings, generals, or famous names. It belongs to every person and every community that chooses to act with purpose, leave something real behind, and contribute to the arc of human progress.

⚡

History is made by those who act, not only those who watch

Every era of history has been shaped by a minority of people who chose to act decisively while others observed. Making history does not require permission — it requires courage, commitment, and the willingness to begin before the outcome is certain.

ናይ ኩሉ ዘመን ታሪኽ ዝቐርጸ ዝሰርሑ — ታሪኽ ምስራሕ ፍቓድ ዘየድልዮ ።

🤝

Collective action writes the boldest chapters

The most enduring chapters of history are written by communities, not lone individuals. When people unite around a shared purpose and act in concert, they generate a force that outlasts any single lifetime. Our collective history is still being written.

ዘለዓለም ናይ ታሪኽ ምዕራፍ ዝጸሓፉ ማሕበረሰባት — ሓቢርና ዝጸሓፎ ዘለዓለም ።

🏛️

Legacy is built in the present, not the future

Legacy is not something we plan for the future — it is the accumulation of what we do today, this year, in this generation. Every action taken with integrity, every institution built with care, every young person developed with intention — these are the bricks of historical legacy, laid now.

ውርሻ ሎሚ ዝሃነጽ — ጽባሕ ዘይኮነ ። ሎሚ ዝሰርሖ ናይ ታሪኽ ዕዳጋ ።

II
ንምንታይ ታሪኽ · Why It Matters

Why Our Existence Must Make History

Existence without contribution fades. The communities that endure across centuries are those that chose to create — not merely consume — the conditions of their time. Here is why making history is not optional for us.

📜
Those who make no history are written out of it

History is not neutral. It is written by those who acted — and it tends to omit those who did not. A community that does not deliberately shape the record of its time will find that record shaped by others, often inaccurately, often unjustly. Making history is also an act of self-definition.

ታሪኽ ዝሰርሑ ዝጽሕፍዎ — ዘይሰርሑ ዝጠፍኡ ። ታሪኽ ምስራሕ ናይ ርእስኻ ምቕራጽ ።

🌱
We owe future generations a foundation to stand on

We did not arrive here without the sacrifices and achievements of those who came before us. We owe the same to those who will come after. The foundation we build — in culture, in institutions, in knowledge, in values — is what determines the range of possibility available to our children and grandchildren.

ናይ ዝቕጽሉ ወለዶ ሓላፍነት ዕዳና ። ዝሃነጽናዮ ናይ ደቅናን ኣደቅቁናን ዓቕሚ ።

🔥
Existence with purpose is the most powerful force

A community that knows why it exists — that has a clear sense of its mission in the world — becomes almost unstoppable. Purpose transforms effort into movement, struggle into sacrifice with meaning, and presence into power. Our existence in the world is not accidental. It is purposeful. And purpose, pursued together, makes history.

ዕቃ ዘለዎ ህላዌ ዝዓቀነ ሓይሊ — ዕቃ ሓቢሩ ታሪኽ ዝሰርሕ ።

III
ከምይ ታሪኽ ምስራሕ · How to Act

How to Make History from Where We Stand

Making history does not begin with greatness — it begins with commitment. These four steps show how any community can move from passive presence to active historical contribution.

1 Define what you are building and for whom

History is made intentionally. The first step is clarity — knowing what you are trying to build, who it serves, and what it will look like when it is done. A community without a defined vision wanders. A community with one moves. Write down what you want your generation to be remembered for. Make it specific. Make it honest. Make it worthy of the effort it will take.

ታሪኽ ብዕቅን ይሰርሕ ። ዕቃ ዘለዎ ዕቅን ዘይብሉ ዕስለ — ዕቃ ዘለዎ ዝሰርሕ ። ወለዶኻ ዘክሮ ዝደሊ ምጻፍ ።

2 Create institutions, not just moments

Moments of inspiration pass. Institutions endure. Making history requires building structures that outlast their founders — schools, organisations, cultural bodies, records, traditions — that carry the community's values and achievements forward across generations. The test of historical impact is not whether something happened, but whether it left a structure that continued to matter.

ናይ ኣምሳለ ህሞታት ይሓልፉ ። ትካላት ዝጸንሑ ። ናይ ታሪኻዊ ምልክት መፈተሽ — ዝሰርሖ ዘይኮነ — ዝቐጸለ ዝሓዞ ።

3 Document, celebrate, and transmit what is achieved

History that is not recorded disappears. Communities that make history also take responsibility for telling it — accurately, proudly, and continuously. Document what is built. Celebrate milestones. Teach the young what has been achieved and why it matters. A people that knows its own story has the foundation to keep writing it.

ዘይተጸሓፈ ታሪኽ ይጠፍእ ። ዝሃነጽናዮ ምጽሓፍ ። ናይ ወሓዙ ዝተዓወተ ምምሃር ።

4 Act with the long arc in mind

Historical action is not measured in days or even years — it is measured in decades and generations. This means resisting the temptation of short-term thinking, making decisions that will look right thirty years from now, investing in people and projects whose full fruit may not be seen in our lifetime. Acting with the long arc in mind is the discipline that separates those who make history from those who merely live through it.

ታሪኻዊ ስጕምቲ ብሰብዓን ወለዶን ዝምዘን ። ናይ ነዊሕ ቀስቲ ምሓሳብ ታሪኽ ዝሰርሑ ካብ ዝሓልፍዎ ዝፈልይ ።

🌍

ህላዌና ኣብዚ ዓለም ዝሕለፍ ዘይኮነ ። ታሪኽ ክንሰርሕ ኢና — ሓቢርና ።

Our existence in this world is not passive. We are here to make history — and we will.

Life Is Honour — The Wisdom Formula: Having Skill, Learning, Developing, and Refining

Life operates by a formula — like a calculator of wisdom. That formula is skill: having it, learning it, developing it, and continuously refining it. We must learn and develop this formula — and if we do not yet have it, we are obligated to acquire it.

ሂወት ክብረት እያ —

ናይ ጥበብ ፎርሙላ ናይ ኳልኩሌተር ።

ክእለት ምህላው ፣ ምምሃር ፣ ምምዕባልን ፣ ክእለት ምጥራይ እያ ።

ስለዚ ክእለት ኣገባብ ናይ ሂወት ክንመሃርን ከነማዕብልን ኣለና ፣

እንተ ዘይ ሃልዩና ግን ክንመሃሮን ከነማዕብሎን ግዴታ ክህልወና ኣለዎ ።

Life is honour — the wisdom formula of a calculator. It is: having skill, learning, developing, and refining skill. Therefore we must learn and develop the formula of life as a skill — and if we do not have it, we must be obligated to learn and develop it.

I
ናይ ጥበብ ፎርሙላ · The Formula

The Four Elements of Life's Formula

A calculator applies its formula precisely and without exception. Life works the same way. The formula has four elements — and all four are required. Missing any one of them produces the wrong result, no matter how hard you try with the others.

🎯

Having skill — the foundation

The first element of the formula is possession — having a skill at all. Not an idea of a skill, not a desire for one, but actual capability: something you can do, produce, and apply. Without this foundation, the rest of the formula has nothing to build on. Every great result begins with someone who actually had a skill.

ፎርሙላ ቀዳማይ ክፍሊ — ክእለት ምህላው ። ሓሳብ ዘይኮነ — ሓቀኛ ዓቕሚ ። ዝሰርሕ፡ ዘፍሪ፡ ዝዛወር ። ዘይብሉ ዝቕጽሎ ዝህነጽ ምስሊ ።

📚

Learning — the engine of growth

Having a skill is not enough if it is static. The second element is learning — the ongoing acquisition of new capability and deeper understanding. Learning is what keeps skill alive and growing. Those who stop learning do not stay still — they fall behind a world that keeps moving.

ክእለት ምህላው ዕቤት ዘምጽእ ኣይኮነን — ምምሃር ዘዓቢ ። ምምሃር ቀዛፊ ። ዘቋርጽ ዘይቐርፍ — ዝቕጽሉ ዓለም ዘሕልፎ ።

⚙️

Developing — turning learning into capability

Learning without development is theory without practice. The third element is deliberate development — taking what you have learned and building it into deeper, broader, and more reliable capability through consistent application. Development is what turns raw learning into genuine skill.

ምምሃር ብዘይ ምምዕባል ንድፊ ። ምምዕባል ዝተምሃርካዮ ናብ ሓቀኛ ዓቕሚ ዘቕልብ ።

💎

Refining — the path to mastery

Why Skill Is Not Optional — It Is a Duty

The statement is clear: if we do not have skill, we are obligated to learn and develop it. This is not a suggestion. Here is why skill — and the pursuit of it — is a moral and collective duty.

🛡️
Unskilled people cannot honour life

Life is called honour — ክብረት — because it has inherent worth and demands to be treated accordingly. A life lived without skill is a life that cannot fully honour itself. You cannot build what you do not know how to build. You cannot contribute what you have not developed. Skill is how we make life worthy of the honour its name demands.

ሂወት ክብረት ትበሃል — ክብረት ዘለዋ ። ብዘይ ክእለት ዝነብር ሂወቱ ብሓቂ ዘኽብሩ ኣይክእሉን ። ክእለት ሂወት ናብ ክብረታ ዘኽብሩ ።

Communities depend on the skill of their members

The world does not wait for the unskilled

A community or individual that does not develop skill will find the world's opportunities progressively out of reach. The obligation to develop skill is also, therefore, an act of self-preservation.

ከምይ ክእለት ምምዕባል · How to Build Skill

How to Learn, Develop, and Refine Life's Formula

The formula is known. The steps to apply it are practical. Whether you already have skill and need to refine it, or are starting from nothing and need to acquire it, these four steps apply equally.

1 Identify exactly which skill is needed

The formula begins with honesty: what skill do you actually need — not what sounds impressive, not what others have, but what your situation, your purpose, and your community genuinely require? Be specific. "I need to communicate better" is too vague. "I need to be able to write clearly, speak persuasively in public, and listen without interrupting" is a formula you can act on. Clarity about which skill is needed is the precondition for everything that follows.

ፎርሙላ ብሓቀኛ ይጅምር — ዘድሊ ክእለት ምፍላይ ። ናትካ ኩነት፡ ዕላማ፡ ማሕበረሰብ ዘድሊ ። ዝዓቀነ ። ምፍላጥ ናይ ዘድሊ ክእለት ናይ ዝቕጽሎ ቅሩቡ ።

2 Learn from those who already have it

The fastest and most reliable path to skill is learning from those who already possess it. Find mentors, teachers, examples, and communities of practice. Study how they think, not just what they do. Ask questions about the reasoning behind actions, not just the actions themselves. The formula of their skill — how they developed it, refined it, and apply it — is available to be learned. Most people with genuine skill are willing to share it with those who ask seriously.

ናብ ዝዓቀነ ክእለት ቅልጡፍ መንገዲ — ካብ ዘለዎ ምምሃር ። መምህር፡ ኣምሳለ፡ ናይ ልምዲ ማሕበረሰብ ምርካብ ። ዝሓስቡ — ዝሰርሑ ዘይኮነ ።

3 Apply it deliberately and repeatedly

Skill is not built by understanding — it is built by doing. The third step is deliberate, repeated application of what you are learning. Not random practice, but focused practice: working on the specific elements where you are weakest, pushing slightly beyond your current comfort zone each time, and treating every application as data — what worked, what did not, and what to adjust. This is how learning becomes development, and development becomes capability.

ክእለት ብምርዳእ ዘይሃነጽ — ብምስርሑ ። ዝዓቀነ ዝደጋገም ምትግባር ። ዝዓቅነ — ተኸዲኻ ዘይኮነ ። ኩሉ ምትግባር ሓበሬታ ።

4 Refine continuously — never consider the formula complete

The final step is the one most people skip: continuous refinement. Seek feedback actively — from people who will tell you the truth, not those who will tell you what you want to hear. Reflect on your own performance honestly. Identify the small adjustments that make the biggest differences. The calculator analogy is exact here: a formula that is even slightly wrong produces systematically wrong results. Refinement is the ongoing correction that keeps the formula accurate, sharp, and producing the outcomes life deserves.

ምጥራይ ብዙሓት ዝስዕብዎ ። ሓቀኛ ምምርማር ምሕታት — ሓቂ ዝነግሩ ካብ ዝምሽጥሩ ። ናይ ካልኩሌተር ፎርሙላ ዝጋጠ — ቀጻሊ ጌጋ ። ምጥራይ ፎርሙላ ሓቀኛ ዘቐርፎ ።

🧮

ሂወት ክብረት እያ — ፎርሙላኣ ክእለት ምህላው ፣ ምምሃር ፣ ምምዕባልን ፣ ምጥራይን ። እዚ ፎርሙላ ናይ ዝነብር ኩሉ ።

Life is honour. Its formula is skill — having it, learning it, developing it, refining it. This formula belongs to everyone willing to apply it.

What the Craft-First Principle Actually Means

How to Organise Your Life around Profession, Not Money

When resources are available — time, money, attention — invest them in professional development rather than financial schemes. Take the course. Find the mentor. Do the hard project that stretches your capability. Read deeply in your field. The professional who consistently reinvests in their own mastery is building compound interest in the form of skill — and compound interest in skill eventually produces financial returns that no shortcut can match.

3 Measure yourself by craft standards, not income comparisons

One of the most damaging habits of money-first thinking is comparing income with peers rather than craft with masters. The right comparison is not "who earns more?" but "how does my work compare to the best in my field?" That comparison — honest, humbling, and constantly recalibrated — is what drives the professional growth that eventually produces superior outcomes, financial and otherwise. Measure yourself against the craft, not against the paycheck.

ናይ ጥበብ ቅደም-ሰዓት ምምዛን — ናይ ሰልዲ ምዛናት ዘይኮነ ። ናብ ጥበብ ምምዛን — ናብ ደሞዝ ዘይኮነ ።

Awareness without action becomes its own corruption

To see clearly what a corrupt system has done — and then to do nothing — is itself a form of complicity. The person who sees and acts is an agent of change. How We Respond to What Was Taken from Us

The response to a corrupt system that stunted our country and people is not rage — it is relentless, disciplined, collective effort. These four steps show how to turn the pain of awareness into the power of reconstruction.

1 Name what was taken — clearly and without minimising

The first step is honest accounting — not to feed grievance, but to understand the scale of what must be rebuilt. What development was prevented? What learning was denied? What technology was withheld? What institutions were corrupted or destroyed? The clearer the inventory of what was taken, the more precise and effective the effort to rebuild it. Communities that vaguely resent their past cannot rebuild it strategically. Communities that understand it precisely can.

If the system denied our people education and technology, the response is to learn with a hunger that reflects that denial. Every course available must be pursued. Every technology that was withheld must now be learned and mastered. This is not merely self-improvement — it is a political act: reclaiming what was ours by right. The individual who learns in a system that tried to keep them ignorant is performing an act of defiance and reconstruction simultaneously.

What a corrupt system refused to build for the many, the people must build for themselves — and together. Schools that were not built must now be built by communities. Skills that were not taught must now be passed from those who have them to those who do not. Technology that was not provided must now be acquired, shared, and developed by the community itself. The corrupt system proved that waiting for a corrupt authority is futile. The answer is to stop waiting and to start building.

ርኲስ ስርዓት ዘይሃነጾ — ህዝቢ ሓቢሩ ዝሃናጾ ። ዝተኸልከለ ሞያ ዘለዎ ናብ ዘይብሉ ። ጽብጽቦ ኣቕርቦ ። ምጽባይ ኣቋርጽ ። ምሃናጽ ጀምር ።

The final step is the most forward-looking: refusing to pass the effects of corruption to the next generation. This means not only building institutions and learning skills, but consciously raising the young with a different set of expectations — that they will learn, that they will master technology, that their country can and will develop, and that they are the agents of that development. Every child raised with this expectation is a brick in the wall of a future the corrupt system tried to prevent.

A corrupt system held our country and our people back. We will not sleep on this. We are awake — and we are building.

ዕቅን ዘለዎ ዕቅን · What This Commitment Means

What It Means to Have No Sleep Until the Goal

"No sleep" is a declaration of inner orientation — not a rejection of rest, but a refusal to let comfort become a substitute for accomplishment. It means the mind stays on the target, the body returns to the work, and the spirit does not accept settlement before the goal is reached.

🎯

Goals are not optional — they are the measure of a life

A life without defined goals drifts. A people without collective targets disperses. The statement "until we hit our goals" presupposes that goals exist — clear, specific, and agreed upon. Before the pursuit can be relentless, the target must be named. Clarity of goal is the precondition for intensity of effort.

ዕቅን ዘይብሉ ሂወት ዝሳሓት ። ሸቶ ዘይብሉ ሕዝቢ ዝበናኒ ። ቅድሚ ዕቁብ ምምርሻ — ሸቶ ብንጹር ።

🔥

The drive must outlast the difficulty

Every meaningful goal encounters resistance — obstacles, setbacks, periods of slow progress. The declaration of "no sleep" is the pre-commitment that the drive will outlast the difficulty. It is the internal contract signed before the hard part arrives: I will not stop because it is hard. I will stop only when it is done.

ዝዓቀነ ሸቶ ኩሉ ዕቅን ይምህሮ ። ሸቶ ከይሃረምና ዕቁብ ምምርሻ — ቅድሚ ምቅዋሙ ዝተፈረመ ቃል ።

⚡

Collective pursuit multiplies the force of individual will

When one person declares "no sleep until the goal," that is determination. When a community declares it together, that is a movement. Collective relentless pursuit creates accountability — each member's continued effort lifts and holds the others. The goal becomes not only personal but shared, and shared goals generate a force that individual willpower alone cannot sustain.

ሓደ ሰብ ዝደምጽዖ ሸቶ — ቈራጽነት ። ሓቢሩ ዝደምጽዎ — ምቅስቃስ ። ሓቢርና ዝሳቐዮ ሸቶ ዘይዕርፍ ሓይሊ ።

Why We Cannot Rest Before the Goal Is Struck

The world rewards those who persist. The goals worth having are defended by difficulty — and only those who refuse to stop before completion claim them. Here is why the intensity of "no sleep" is not extreme — it is simply accurate.

Unfinished goals compound into unfinished lives

A goal abandoned halfway does not merely leave a task incomplete — it leaves a pattern. The habit of stopping before the finish line, once established, repeats across every domain. The declaration of "no sleep" is the refusal to establish that pattern — it is the insistence that what we begin, we complete, and that the standard of completion is the goal itself, not comfort.

ዘይተወደአ ሸቶ ቅርጺ ዝፈጥር ። ናብ ሓዊሳ ቅርጺ ። "ድቃስ የብልናን" — ቅርጺ ዘይምምሃሩ ።

The final stretch is where most goals are abandoned

Research and experience both confirm: the most common point of abandonment is not at the beginning, when the difficulty is anticipated, nor at the middle, when momentum carries — it is near the end, when the effort has accumulated and the goal is almost in reach. The declaration of "no sleep" is most important precisely at this point: when the finish line is visible but the body wants to stop.

ዝበዝሑ ሸቶ ዝተሓደጉ ቅርብ ናብ መወዳእታ ። ሸቶ ዝርኤ ኣካል ምስ ዝደቅስ ። ሕጂ ዝዓቢ ቃልሲ ።

Arriving at the goal transforms the person who arrives

The reason to pursue relentlessly is not only the goal itself — it is what the pursuit does to the one who pursues it. To set a difficult target, to fight through the resistance, and to arrive — this is the process that builds genuine capacity, genuine confidence, and a proven record of completion. The goal transforms the person who achieves it into someone capable of setting and achieving the next one.

ከምይ ሸቶ ምሃራም · How to Pursue

How to Pursue Without Rest Until the Goal Is Hit

Relentless pursuit is not chaos — it is disciplined focus. These four steps show how to sustain the intensity of "no sleep" over the weeks, months, and years that meaningful goals require.

1Define the goal with enough precision to know when it is hit

You cannot hit a target you cannot see. Before the pursuit begins, define the goal with enough specificity that you will know, without ambiguity, when it has been reached. Vague goals produce vague effort and vague results. Specific goals produce measurable progress and definitive arrival. "We want to develop" is not a goal. "We will establish three functional community learning centres by the end of this year" is a goal you can pursue relentlessly — because you know exactly what hitting it looks like.

ዘይርኤ ሸቶ ዘይሃረምካዮ ። ብዕምቆት ምፍላይ — ምስ ዝዛዘምካ ትፈልጦ ። ዘይዓቀነ ሸቶ ዘይዓቀነ ጥረ ።

2Break the goal into daily and weekly actions

A goal that exists only at the destination is unreachable from the present. Break every major goal into the specific actions required today, this week, and this month. The pursuit of "no sleep" is not sustained by thinking about the destination — it is sustained by acting on the next step. Daily action is the engine of relentless pursuit. Weekly review is the steering wheel. Monthly assessment ensures the direction remains correct.

ሸቶ ናብ ዕለታዊ ምፍሓቑ ። ዕለታዊ ስጕምቲ ናይ ዕቁብ ምምርሻ ቀዛፊ ። ሰሙናዊ ምፍታሽ መቐይሮ ።

3Build accountability structures that hold you to the pursuit

Individual willpower is finite. Structures are not. Build accountability mechanisms that hold the pursuit in place even when personal motivation fluctuates — partners who check progress, public commitments that cost something to break, regular review rituals that surface what is and is not happening. Relentless pursuit is not about having unlimited energy; it is about building systems that keep returning you to the work regardless of how you feel that day.

ናይ ውልቀ ሓይሊ ዝውዳእ ። ቅርጺ ዘይውዳእ ። ሓብርቲ ዝፍትሽ፡ ቃል ዋጋ ዘለዎ — ቀዋሚ ናብ ስርሑ ዘምለስ ።

When the goal is hit — celebrate it fully. Mark it, honour it, name what was achieved and who achieved it. This is not vanity; it is the renewal of the motivation that will sustain the next pursuit. Then, without long delay, define the next target. The people who live by "no sleep until the goal" do not rest in achievement — they recognise that arrival at one goal is simply the starting line of the next. This is the cycle that builds extraordinary communities.

🎯

ዕላማና ሸቶናን ከይሃረምና ድቃስ የብልናን — ሸቶ ምስ ዘሃረምና ዝቕጽሉ ንሃርም ።

Governance vacuums attract the worst, not the best

Power does not sit empty. When those with integrity refuse to participate in governance — local or national — those without integrity step in. Disengagement is a vote for the current system

ዝቕጽሉ ሰብ ዘይሳተፍ — ዝቕጽሉ ስርዓት ዘሰጓጉም ። ምርሓቕ ምኽባር ዘይኮነ — ናይ ዝቕጽሉ ምርጫ ።

Every generation must re-earn its country

ከምይ ሃገር ምሕላው · How to Reclaim and Hold

How to Prevent the Country Falling to Bandits and Opportunists

Reclaiming and holding a country from corrupt governance is not a single dramatic act — it is the daily, organised, patient work of civic engagement. These four steps show how that work is done.

1Participate — in every institution, at every level

The first act of civic responsibility is showing up. In community meetings, in local organisations, in professional bodies, in public processes — wherever decisions are made that affect the community, principled and capable people must be present. Not as passive observers but as active participants who ask questions, propose alternatives, and hold decision-makers accountable. The presence of capable, honest people in every room where decisions are made is the first line of defence against capture.

ቀዳማይ ሕዝባዊ ሓላፍነት ምቕራቡ ። ኣብ ኩሉ ዝውሰን — ዝሳተፍ ። ዘይዕዘብ — ዝሰርሕ ። ሕዝቢ ዝቐርፎ ቀዳማይ ምክልካሉ ።

2Build and strengthen civic institutions

Individual engagement is necessary but not sufficient. The protection of a community from corrupt governance requires strong institutions — organisations, bodies, and structures that persist beyond individual people, that carry the community's values and interests forward, and that can withstand pressure from those who want to exploit them. Build these institutions. Strengthen existing ones. Defend them when they are attacked. The country is as strong as its civic institutions — and those institutions are as strong as the people committed to building and defending them.

ናይ ውልቀ ምሳተፍ ዘኣክል ዘይኮነ ። ዝጸንሑ ትካላት ምሃናጽ ። ናይ ሕዝቢ ዋጋ ዝሕዙ ። ሃገር ናይ ሕዝባዊ ትካሉ ብርታዕ ።

3Hold every leader accountable — without exception

Accountability is the immune system of good governance. Communities that hold their leaders accountable — demanding transparency, insisting on results, removing those who betray the trust placed in them — are communities that are resistant to capture by bandits and opportunists. The moment accountability is suspended — for any leader, for any reason — the door to corruption opens. Accountability must be consistent, public, and independent of who is being held to account.

4Develop principled leaders from within the community

The long-term solution to corrupt governance is not only to remove bad leaders — it is to develop good ones. Communities must deliberately identify, mentor, prepare, and elevate people of integrity and capability into positions of leadership — at every level, from community organisations to national institutions. This is not left to chance; it is a deliberate programme of civic development. The country is what its leaders make of it — and leaders are what communities invest in making.

እትጸልእን እትሰጉግን ስርዓት —

ካብ ሱራ ክትብንቆስ ኣለዋ ።

A system that hates and expels educated and intelligent people — must be uprooted from its very roots.

I
ስርዓት ዘጽልእ · What Such a System Does

What a System That Expels the Educated Actually Is

A system that persecutes the educated and expels the intelligent is not an accident of incompetence — it is a deliberate structure of self-preservation. It recognises that knowledge is power and responds by trying to eliminate those who have it. Understanding this clearly is the first step to dismantling it.

🚫

It expels because it fears what knowledge produces

A corrupt system expels the educated not because educated people are dangerous in themselves, but because of what education produces: the capacity to see clearly, to analyse without deception, to organise with precision, and to hold power accountable. These are the exact capabilities that a system built on exploitation cannot survive. The expulsion of the educated is the system's confession of its own illegitimacy.

ርኲስ ስርዓት ዝተምህሩ ዘሰጓጉ ፍልጠት ዘምጽኦ ዝፈርህ ። ምሳቐጡ ናይ ርኲስ ስርዓት ናይ ናቱ ዘይሕጋዊ ምምቅቡ ።

🧠

It hates intelligence because intelligence challenges it

The system does not hate the educated person — it hates what that person represents: the existence of a standard against which the system's own performance can be judged and found inadequate. Intelligence, critical thought, and honest analysis are existential threats to a system that depends on confusion, ignorance, and manufactured consent. The hatred is not personal; it is structural and predictable.

ስርዓት ዝጸልእ ሰብ ዘይኮነ — ዘምጽኦ ቅርጺ ። ፍልጠት ናብ ርኲስ ስርዓት ዝዓቅን ሓደጋ ።

🌿

Uprooting requires going to the roots — not trimming the branches

The statement does not say "reform" or "improve" — it says uproot. ካብ ሱራ ምብንቃስ — from the very roots. This is a recognition that a system designed to expel knowledge and suppress intelligence cannot be corrected by adjusting its surface features. The root assumptions — that power is for extraction, that knowledge is a threat, that the capable must be silenced — must be destroyed and replaced, not amended.

ካብ ሱራ ምብንቃስ — ምምሕያሽ ዘይኮነ ። ናይ ሱሩ ሓሳብ ምድምሳስ ። ምምሕያሽ ኣይኮነ — ምብንቃስ ።

II
ምኽንያት · Why Uprooting Is Necessary

Why Such a System Cannot Be Left in Place

A system that expels the educated does not merely harm the individuals it expels — it amputates the very capacity its community needs to grow, to compete, and to survive in a complex world. Here is why uprooting is not radical — it is the minimum responsible response.

📉
It condemns the community to managed ignorance

When the educated are expelled and the intelligent are suppressed, the community is left with a systematically degraded knowledge base. Over time, the decisions made — in governance, in health, in education, in economics — are made without the benefit of the best available thinking. The result is not merely inefficiency but compounding failure: each poor decision creates the conditions for worse ones, until recovery requires extraordinary effort.

ዝተምህሩ ምስ ዝሰጋጉ — ማሕበረሰብ ብዘይ ዝሓሸ ሓሳብ ። ናይ ምሕደራ ሕቶ ። ዝዓቀነ ጌጋ ዝዓቀነ ጌጋ ።

✈️
It exports the country's greatest asset

The educated and the intelligent are a community's most valuable asset — the people capable of building institutions, solving complex problems, teaching the next generation, and driving innovation. When a system expels them, it does not merely lose their contribution — it exports it. They build elsewhere. They contribute elsewhere. Their country pays the cost of developing them and receives none of the return.

ዝተምህሩ ዝሰጋጉ ኣብ ካልእ ዝሃናጹ ። ሃገሮም ዋጋ ዝኸፍሉ — ምምለስ ዘይርእዩ ። ዝዓቀነ ሃብቲ ምስደዱ ።

🔓
Its removal creates the conditions for everything else

Every other goal — development, technological advancement, civic health, justice, prosperity — depends on a system that rewards knowledge rather than punishes it. The uprooting of a system that expels the educated is therefore not one goal among many — it is the precondition for all the others. Until such a system is removed from its roots, every other effort operates under a ceiling it cannot break through.

ናይ ዝሃቀናዮ ኩሉ — ምምዕባሉ፡ ቴክኖሎጂ፡ ፍትሒ — ስርዓት ፍልጠት ዘሸልም ዘደልዮ ። ምብንቃስ ናይ ኩሉ ቅሩቡ ።

III
ከምይ ካብ ሱራ ምብንቃስ · How to Uproot

How to Uproot a System That Punishes Knowledge

Uprooting is not a single act of force — it is a sustained, multi-layered process of delegitimisation, replacement, and reconstruction. These four steps describe what uprooting actually requires.

1Name the system clearly — and refuse to normalise it

A system that expels the educated survives partly because those within it learn to treat its pathologies as normal — to accept expulsion, suppression, and the punishment of intelligence as simply how things are. The first act of uprooting is refusal: the refusal to normalise, to excuse, or to accommodate. Name what the system is doing. Name it accurately and publicly. The loss of normalcy is the beginning of the loss of legitimacy — and without legitimacy, no system can hold.

ዝሰጓጉ ስርዓት ብሓቀኛ ምስምያቱ — ሰናዪ ዘይምብሃሉ ። ሰናዪ ምምሃዝ ምኽልካሉ ። ምቕባሉ ናይ ምጭብጨቡ ጀምር ።

2Build parallel structures that reward knowledge

While the corrupt system is being delegitimised, build alongside it — or in the spaces it leaves — institutions and structures that do the opposite: that invite the educated, reward intelligence, develop the capable, and share knowledge freely. These parallel structures serve two purposes: they provide real benefit to the community now, and they model what the replacement system will look like. They are simultaneously the solution and the proof that a better alternative exists.

ርኲስ ስርዓት ዝሕደሩ ትካላት ምሃናጽ — ዝተምህሩ ዘዕድሙ ። ናይ ምትካእ ምስሊ ።

3Protect, retain, and recall the expelled

Every educated and intelligent person the system has expelled is a resource the community cannot afford to lose permanently. Where possible, work to keep the expelled within reach of the community — through networks, through communication, through shared projects that allow them to contribute even from a distance. Build the conditions that will make return possible and desirable. The community that can recall its expelled minds when the system changes will recover faster and build stronger than one that has permanently lost them.

ዝሰጉጉ ካብ ናይ ማሕበረሰብ ዕቃ ዘይምጥፍኦ ። ዝምልከቶ ። ሓቢሩ ምስርሑ — ካብ ርሑቕ'ውን ። ምምለስ ናይ ዝቕርቦ ።

4Replace the roots — not only the leaders

The final step — and the one most often skipped — is the replacement of the root assumptions, not only the surface personnel. A system that expels the educated can change its leaders and keep its logic. True uprooting replaces the logic: from "knowledge is a threat" to "knowledge is our greatest asset"; from "the capable must be controlled" to "the capable must be empowered"; from "loyalty to the system" to "loyalty to the community." These are not slogans — they are the specific, operational values that must be built into every institution that replaces what was there before.

ናይ መወዳእታ ደረጃ — ሱር ምትካእ ፡ ኣቦ ጥራሕ ዘይኮነ ። ናይ ሱሩ ሓሳብ: ፍልጠት ሓደጋ ዘይኮነ — ዝዓቀነ ሃብቲ ። ዝዓቀነ ትካል ።

🌿

ንዝተምህሩን በሉሕትን ዘጽልእ ስርዓት ካብ ሱራ ክትብንቆስ ኣለዋ — ብፍልጠት፡ ብዝተወደበ ጻዕሪ፡ ሓቢርና ።

A system that hates and expels the educated must be uprooted from its roots — through knowledge, through organised effort, and together.

Their sacrifice demands the country they imagined

Remembering the betrayal is not bitterness — it is fuel

ደው ምባል · What Standing Together Means

What This Commitment Requires

"Standing by our people" is not a feeling — it is a practice. It is a daily choice to remain connected, to take action, and to refuse to accept oppression and hunger as permanent conditions. Three things define what genuine solidarity looks like.

🤝

Solidarity is active — not spectatorial

To stand by the oppressed is not to observe their suffering with sympathy — it is to act. To contribute resources, skills, and presence to the work of change. To build what is needed, to speak what is true, to organise what is required. The commitment "we will stand beside them" is a verb, not a noun. It demands movement, not sentiment.

ምጽዋር ምዕዛብ ዘይኮነ — ምስርሑ ። ሃቢ ። ዘዛሪ ። ዝወደብ ። ዝቋቆም ። ግብሪ ።

Free education is not a luxury — it is the floor of justice

The commitment to stand by the people until they have "free education" is a commitment to the most foundational condition of human freedom. A person who cannot access education is not free in any meaningful sense — they are locked out of the tools required to understand, to choose, and to act in their own interest. Until every person has access to quality education regardless of their economic condition, the work is not done.

ናጻ ትምህርቲ ምቕሻጥ ዘይኮነ — ናብ ፍትሒ ። ዘይምምሃር ናይ ሓርነት ምኽላእ ። ናጻ ። ዋጋ ዘይብሉ ።

A person who cannot work freely cannot build a dignified life. Standing with the people means standing until this right is not a privilege but the universal condition of every working person.

ናጻ ስርሒ ናይ ሂወት ። ዘይብሉ — ዘይሃናጽ ። ናጻ ። ዋጋ ዘይብሉ ። ዘይተቐይቐ ።

ምኽንያት · Why Until the End

Why the Commitment Is "Until the End"

The phrase "until the end" — ክሳብ መወዳእታ — is deliberate. It means the commitment does not expire when it becomes inconvenient. It does not come with conditions or escape clauses. Here is why that unconditional commitment is the only kind that matters.

⏳
Conditional solidarity abandons people at their lowest point

Solidarity that expires when the cause becomes difficult or the timeline extends is not solidarity — it is convenience. The moment a suffering people most needs support is precisely the moment when conditions are hardest, when hope is lowest, and when the temptation to step back is strongest. "Until the end" is the declaration that we will not step back at that moment — that our commitment does not depend on how quickly the goal is achieved.

ምጽዋር ምስ ዝጽበ ዝዛዘም ዘይኮነ ምጽዋር ። ሕጂ ዘድሊ ። ዝሓሸ ። ክሳብ መወዳእታ ።

🌊
Change requires sustained pressure, not episodic bursts

Oppressive systems are durable. They are designed to outlast episodic resistance — to wait out the surge of outrage, to survive the momentary wave of solidarity, and to reassert control when attention moves elsewhere. The only pressure that works against a durable system is pressure that is equally durable. "Until the end" is the commitment to provide exactly that: sustained, organised, relentless pressure that the system cannot simply wait out.

ርኲስ ስርዓት ዝጸንሕ ። ዝሓለፈ ሰሊቡ ዝቕጽሉ ። ዝጸንሕ ጸቕጢ ። ክሳብ መወዳእታ ።

🌟
Together multiplies what alone cannot do

The closing word is ብሓባር — together. Not because any single person lacks courage or commitment, but because what solidarity requires cannot be sustained alone. The burden of standing with the oppressed until the end is too heavy for individuals to carry alone — it requires communities, networks, and organised collectives that distribute the weight and multiply the force. Together is not an addition to the commitment; it is the condition that makes the commitment possible.

ብሓባር — ናይ ምጽዋር ቅርጺ ። ዘይውልቀ ። ዝወደበ ። ዝዓቀነ ሓይሊ ።

III
ከምይ ደው ምባል · How to Stand

How to Stand with the People Until the End

The commitment is made — now the question is practice. How does "standing with the people" translate into consistent, effective, and sustained action over time?

1Stay connected — maintain the link to the people's reality

The first discipline of solidarity is staying informed about the actual condition of the people you stand with — not through filtered narratives or comfortable abstractions, but through direct connection. Listen. Ask. Read what they write, hear what they say, understand what they face. Solidarity that drifts from the reality of the people it claims to support becomes performative — it serves the supporter's conscience rather than the people's needs. Stay connected, and let that connection shape every action.

ቀዳማይ ናይ ምጽዋር ስምዓን ምስ ህዝቢ ። ብቐጥታ ። ዘይፈቅደ ። ዘሰምዕ ። ዘርዳ ። ዝምርሸሉ ።

2Contribute what you have — skill, time, resource, voice

Standing with the people is not possible at a safe distance with empty hands. Every person who stands in solidarity must contribute what they have — whether that is professional skill brought to a community need, time given to organising and education, material resources shared with those who have less, or the voice used publicly to speak the truth that others are afraid to say. Contribution does not require abundance — it requires the willingness to offer what you have in service of the commitment.

ምጽዋር ካብ ርሑቕ ዘይኮነ ። ዋሃቢ ። ሞያ ። ጊዜ ። ሃብቲ ። ድምጺ ። ዝርከቦ ።

3Organise — build the structures that outlast individuals

Individual commitment, however sincere, is fragile — it is subject to fatigue, circumstance, and the limits of a single person's capacity. Solidarity that lasts "until the end" requires organisation: structures, networks, and institutions that hold the commitment in place independent of any single individual's continued capacity. Build these structures. Join them. Strengthen them. The commitment to stand with the people is only as durable as the organisations that carry it forward when individuals are unable to continue alone.

ናይ ውልቀ ቃል ዝሰበር ። ዝተወደበ ዘይሰበር ። ምጽዋር ምህናጽ ቅርጺ ። ናይ ዝቕጽሉ ።

4Model the world you are building — now

Standing with the oppressed until they are free does not mean waiting for freedom to arrive before beginning to live its values. Start now: treat every person with the dignity that the future we are building promises. Share knowledge freely as if the free education we demand for everyone already exists. Create work and opportunity as if the free and dignified labour we fight for is already a reality in the spaces we control. The world we are building begins in how we treat each other today — and that example is itself a form of solidarity.

ሂወት ናይ ነጻነት — ሕጂ ምጅማር ። ዝሃናጽ ዓለም ናይ ሎሚ ። ዓለም ቅርጺ ।

🤝

ክሳብ መወዳእታ — ኣብ ጎድኒ ህዝብናን ውጹዓትናን ደው ክንብል ኣለና ። ብሓባር ። ክሳብ ሓርነት ።

Until the end — we stand beside our people and the oppressed. Together. Until freedom.

ወለዲ · ኣሕዋት · ኣሓት · Family — Our Sacred Obligation

For Our Parents, Brothers, and Sisters — We Must Struggle and Free Them

This is not an abstract political struggle. It is personal. Our parents, our brothers, our sisters — suffering alone, thirsty, hungry, without clothing, without education, without the dignity of free work. We must struggle for them. We must free them from this treacherous system.

ንወለድናን ንኣሕዋትናን ኣሓትናን —

ኣብ ጎድኖም ከይቆሩን ከይጽምዮምን ፣

ከይጠምዩ ከይዓርቁን ፣

ክመሃሩን ነጻ ስራሕ ክረኽቡን —

ካብ እዛ ኣመንዝራ ስርዓት ነጻ ከነውጽኦም ይግበኣና ።

For our parents, our brothers and sisters — so they do not suffer alone and thirst, so they do not hunger and go without clothing — so they may learn and find free work — we must struggle and free them from this treacherous system.

I
ናይ ስድራ ጭቆና · What Our Families Face

What Our Parents, Brothers, and Sisters Are Living Through

The suffering described is not a list of abstract conditions — it is the daily reality of specific people: our mother, our father, our brother, our sister. Named in this declaration are four specific deprivations that this system has imposed on them. Each one is a crime. Together they are a systematic destruction of human dignity.

🕯️

ኣብ ጎድኖም ቆሪ — Suffering alone in the cold

ቆሪ — the cold — is not only physical. It is the cold of abandonment: of being left without protection, warmth, or the presence of those who should stand beside you. A system that leaves families isolated, without resources, without community support, and without the warmth of a society that sees and cares for them — is a system that has deliberately extinguished the human obligation of solidarity. Our parents and siblings should not face the cold alone. We are the warmth they deserve.

ቆሪ — ኣካላዊ ጥራሕ ዘይኮነ ። ናይ ምሕዛን ቆሪ ። ስርዓት ዘንቀሎ ናይ ስድራ ዛዕባ ።

💧

ጽምኢ — Thirst in every sense

ጽምኢ — thirst — is among the most fundamental deprivations possible. A system that cannot ensure clean water, adequate nutrition, and basic nourishment to its people is not simply failing — it is committing a daily crime against human life. And beyond the physical, thirst is the longing for knowledge, for opportunity, for the dignity of being seen and heard. Our families thirst for all of this. They should not have to beg a system for what they are owed.

ጽምኢ ናብ ዝቐረ ። ማይ ። ፍልጠት ። ምኽባር ። ዕቤት ። ስርዓት ዝኸልኦ ። ዝናፍቕ ።

🍞

ጥሜትን ዕርቂን — Hunger and nakedness

To go hungry — ጥሜት — and to go without clothing — ዕርቂ — are among the oldest definitions of material deprivation. They describe a condition in which a person lacks the most basic means of sustaining life and preserving dignity. No system that produces or tolerates hunger and nakedness among its people can claim legitimacy. These are not the results of natural scarcity — they are the results of deliberate political choices that direct resources away from the people who need them most.

ጥሜትን ዕርቂን — ናይ ምሕደርን ዋጋ ዘይብሉ ። ናይ ሕብረተሰብ ሓጢኣት ። ምርጫ ።

📚

ምምሃርን ነጻ ስራሕን — The right to learn and work freely

Beyond survival — beyond cold, thirst, hunger, and nakedness — there is the deprivation of possibility. The right to learn. The right to work freely. The right to build a life according to one's own capacities and choices. A system that denies these rights does not merely suppress the present — it seals the future. Our parents and siblings deserve not only to survive but to grow, to learn, to work with dignity, and to build lives that are fully their own.

ምምሃርን ነጻ ስርሒን — ናብ ዝቕርብ ። ምምሃር ። ዓቕሚ ። ዝዓቀነ ሂወት ። ቤተሰብ ።

II
ምኽንያት · Why This Is Our Obligation

Why We Are Obligated to Struggle for Our Families

The obligation is not chosen — it is constituted by the relationship itself. To have parents, brothers, and sisters who suffer under a treacherous system and to do nothing is not neutrality — it is abandonment. Here is why the obligation to struggle for them is absolute.

❤️
The bond of family is also a bond of responsibility

To call someone your parent, your brother, your sister is to make a claim — not only of affection but of mutual obligation. The bond that gives you the right to be cared for by them when you needed it creates the reciprocal obligation to stand for them when they need it. That obligation does not expire because the system makes it difficult. It intensifies. The harder the system makes it to stand for our families, the more essential it becomes that we do.

ቤተሰብ ናይ ሓቦ ዘይኮነ — ናይ ሓላፍነት ። ዝለቀ ዝሃቦ ። ዝደለ ሎሚ ። ዝዓቀነ ።

⚖️
Their suffering under this system is ours to end

The system that causes their suffering is not a natural force — it is a human construction that human action can dismantle. When we name it as ኣመንዝራ ስርዓት — a treacherous, immoral system — we are not venting emotion; we are making an accurate assessment of something that has betrayed its fundamental obligation to the people. And the recognition that it is human-made and human-maintained means that it is human action — our action — that can end it.

ዝሳቀዩ ስርዓት ናይ ሰብ ። ናይ ሰብ ዝፈርሶ ። ናቱ ። ናትና ። ሕጂ ።

🔗
Their liberation and ours are inseparable

We cannot be truly free while our parents, brothers, and sisters remain under oppression. The freedom of any member of a family is incomplete when others in that family remain in chains. The struggle to free our families is therefore not separate from the struggle to free ourselves — it is the same struggle. Freedom is not a private achievement; it is a shared condition. Until our families have it, none of us fully has it.

ሓርነትና ሓቢርና ። ስድራ ዝኸደ ሓርነት ። ቤተሰብ ናይ ሓርነቶም — ናቱ ። ሓቢርና ።

III
ከምይ ቃልሲ · How We Struggle for Them

How We Fight for Our Parents, Brothers, and Sisters

The declaration ends with a verb: ንቃለስ — we struggle. ነነጻ ከነውጽኦም — we free them. These are not wishes. They are commitments. Here is what fulfilling those commitments looks like in practice.

1Meet the immediate need — warmth, food, clothing, presence

The struggle for liberation does not wait for the system to fall before it begins to act. While the long work of freeing the people from the system proceeds, the immediate needs of our families must be met now — by us, together, through whatever means we have. Share what you have. Be present with those who are alone. Ensure no parent, no brother, no sister in your reach goes cold, thirsty, or hungry while we have the capacity to prevent it. This is not charity — it is the first act of solidarity made concrete.

ቃልሲ ሕጂ ። ድሕሪ ምርጫ ዘይኮነ ። ስቕ ። ምቕናዕ ። ምሃቢ ። ምህላው ። ሓቢርና ።

2Secure their access to education — now and after liberation

The right to learn — ምምሃር — must not remain a future promise. Begin building and funding access to education for our families now: literacy programmes, vocational training, skills sharing, and every form of structured learning that can be organised within the community independently of the system's obstruction. And in the work of liberation, insist that free universal education is not a secondary demand but a foundational one — the condition without which all other freedoms are incomplete.

ምምሃር ሕጂ ። ናብ ዝርከብ ። ናብ ምምሃር ። ድሕሪ ምርጫ ዘይኮነ ። ሕጂ ። ኣካልዊ ።

3Build paths to free and dignified work

Free work — ነጻ ስራሕ — means work that is chosen, fairly compensated, and free from coercion. Where the system blocks this, build alternatives: cooperative structures, community enterprises, skills networks, and economic arrangements that allow our families to work with dignity within the community's own capacity. Liberation from the system must include liberation into an economy that serves the people — and building that economy begins now, in the spaces the system does not fully control.

ነጻ ስርሒ — ዝተምርጸ ። ናብ ካልኦት ።ናይ ሕዝቢ ናብ ዝሃናጽ ። ሕጂ ። ናብ ዝርከብ ።

4Name the system accurately and organise to dismantle it

ኣመንዝራ ስርዓት — a treacherous, immoral system — must be named as such, publicly and without apology. The naming is not emotional excess; it is an accurate description of a system that has betrayed every person it was supposed to serve. And naming it clearly is the first step toward the organised, collective action required to dismantle it: building the coalitions, the alternative institutions, and the sustained public pressure that will ultimately end its hold on our families and our people. The struggle is not abstract — it is for the specific people we love.

ኣመንዝራ ስርዓት — ብሓቀኛ ምስምያቱ ። ፍሉጥ ። ዘይምስምምዕ ። ዝቃለስ ። ዝወደብ ። ናብ ስድራና ።

❤️

ንወለድናን ኣሕዋትናን ኣሓትናን — ካብ እዛ ኣመንዝራ ስርዓት ነጻ ከነውጽኦም ይግበኣና ። ብሓቂ ። ብቃልሲ ። ሓቢርና ።

For our parents, our brothers and sisters — we must free them from this treacherous system. In truth. In struggle. Together.

Identify the Double-Hearted — Protect Our People from Those Who Seek to Divide and Suppress

People who live only for personal gain — who have no care for peace or the welfare of the people — must be identified early, removed from every position of trust, and prevented from hollowing out our institutions and communities from within.

I
ሰብ ክልተ ልብ · The Double-Hearted

Who Are Those Who Seek to Divide and Suppress Our People

The people described here are not strangers — they live among us, work alongside us, and sometimes lead us. They are not identifiable by appearance but by behaviour: what they do with access, influence, and trust when they have it. Knowing them clearly is the first condition of protecting our communities from their agenda.

🎭

They serve only personal gain — never the community

The double-hearted — ሰብ ክልተ ልብ — present one face in public and pursue a different agenda in private. Their only consistent loyalty is to their own interest and advancement. Every position they hold, every institution they enter is evaluated as a source of personal extraction. They have no sincere commitment to peace, to the people's welfare, or to the common good — and any display of such commitment is a tactic, not a conviction.

ሰብ ክልተ ልብ — ናይ ውልቃዊ ዕቤት ጥራሕ ። ቦታ ናይ ምቁጻጽ ። ሰላምን ህዝቢን — ሜላ ።

⛪

They infiltrate religious institutions to control and corrupt them

One of the most damaging tactics of those who seek to divide is the infiltration of the church and religious institutions. Using threats and manufactured accusations — labelling righteous leaders as heretics — they expel the principled and install the compliant. The church, which should be a place of spiritual integrity, becomes an instrument of political control. True leaders are silenced. The institution is hollowed of its purpose while its name and authority continue to be used.

ቤተ ክርስቲያን ናብ ፖለቲካ ። ቅኑዓት ምስባር ። ናብ ናቶም ።

💰

They use our own resources to fund our division and suffering

The wealth of the church, the wealth of the people, the wealth of the nation — resources that belong to the community — are redirected to fund programmes of scattering, dividing, and suppressing the people. While our families go hungry, their resources are used against them. The people pay, in multiple ways, for their own oppression. The money that should build schools, feed families, and strengthen communities is consumed by an apparatus of control and division.

ሃብቲ ህዝቢ ናብ ምብታን ። ህዝቢ ብጥሜት ። ናቱ ሃብቲ ናብ ጭቆናኡ ።

II
ምኽንያት · Why We Cannot Stay Silent

Why Silence in the Face of This Is Not an Option

The temptation to say 'this is not my problem' or to assume someone else will deal with it is understandable — but it is not responsible. The cost of silence is carried by the entire community. Here is why we must speak, act, and not look away.

📚
They actively prevent people from learning, growing, and living in peace

Those with a dividing agenda do not want the people to grow, to learn, to develop, or to live in peace. An educated, self-reliant people is the greatest threat to their control. So they work specifically to prevent education, to create division, and to undermine peace — keeping the community fragmented and weak. Every day of silence allows that programme to continue.

ህዝቢ ከይዓብን ከይምሃርን — ቀዳምነቶም ። ዝምሃር ዝዓቢ ሕብረተሰብ — ናብ ቁጽጽር ዝፈናቅሎ ።

✝️
A church corrupted by politics becomes a source of harm rather than healing

When a religious institution is captured by political actors, it does not merely stop doing good — it becomes an active instrument of harm. The same trust and authority that allow the church to protect its members are weaponised against those same members: to control speech, to justify expulsions, and to enforce political compliance through religious threat. A corrupted church injures the people it claims to serve.

ቤተ ክርስቲያን ናብ ጭቆና ። ተኣማኒ — ናብ ዓዘቅቲ ። ዝፈናቅሎ ።

⚖️
Using the people's resources against the people is the deepest betrayal

There are many ways to harm a community. Among the most profound is to take what belongs to the community — its wealth, its institutions, its trust — and use those very things against it. When community wealth funds division, when institutional authority funds oppression, when the people's own resources are turned into instruments of their suppression — the betrayal is complete. This is not an accident; it is the deliberate strategy of those who have captured what belongs to the people.

ሃብቲ ህዝቢ ። ናብ ጭቆና ። ዝዓቀ ። ሓቀኛ ። ናይ ምሕዛን ።

🌿
The system does not care about our faith, culture, education, or community — but we must not sleep

The existing system in Eritrea does not care about the survival and flourishing of our religion, our culture, our development, or the education of our community. What it has is personal interest and a hunger for power — nothing more. But we must not sleep in our lives. We must not be passive while what gives life its meaning is being eroded. Because it is with our parents, our community, and our people that the true taste of happiness and joy is found. The system cannot give us that. Only our own active, awake, collective life can. This is why we must rise — not later, but now.

ስርዓት ። ሃይማኖት ። ባህሊ ። ትምህርቲ ። ህዝቢ ። ክንድቅስ የብልናን ። ደስታ ። ምስ ወለዲን ሕብረተሰብን ።

🎓
We have many educated and capable leaders — but are we truly supporting them?

Among our people there are many scholars, experienced elders, and capable individuals. The question we must face honestly is whether we are truly standing behind them. Qualified people cannot lead and serve if the community does not recognise them, lift them up, and actively back their work. A people who possess wisdom but do not invest in those who carry it will find that wisdom going unused — or leaving altogether. Our responsibility is not only to produce capable people but to support them so they can give back to us in full.

ምሁራን ። ዓበይቲ ። ብቅዓት ። ምድጋፍ ። ሓቂ ።

A person who walks will stumble — but will never be defeated

In any meaningful journey there will be obstacles and rough patches. The question is not whether difficulties will come — they will. The question is whether we have the capability to face and overcome them. If we do not yet have that capability, we must strive with everything we have to build it. That striving, that constant effort to grow and fill the gaps within ourselves, is what gives life its meaning. It is also what leads us, step by step, to victory.

ጉዕዞ ። ዕንቅፋት ። ብቅዓት ። ምጽዓር ። ዓወት ።

ከምይ ንፍለጦ ንኣልዮ · How to Identify and Remove

How to Identify, Remove, and Protect Our Communities

This work requires clarity, courage, and collective commitment. It is not the work of one person or one leader — it is the shared responsibility of every member of the community who sees what is happening. These seven steps describe what that responsibility looks like in practice.

1Identify them early — by their actions, not their words

The double-hearted cannot be identified by what they say — they are skilled at saying what the community wants to hear. They are identified by what they do: how they use access to resources, how they treat those who challenge them, whether their actions consistently serve the community or consistently serve themselves, whether they build trust or manufacture division. The community must develop the practice of looking carefully at actions, not only at words.

ብተግባር ። ዘይኮነ ብዘረባ ። ምርኣይ ። ምዝናብ ። ምስምያቱ ።

2Remove them from positions of trust — in every institution

Identification without action is not enough. Once identified, those whose agenda is personal gain at the community's expense must be removed from positions where they can cause harm — from community leadership, from professional roles, from any position that gives them access to the resources and decisions that shape people's lives. This requires organised, collective action: the courage to name the problem publicly and to replace those removed with people of genuine integrity.

ምፍላጥ ጥራሕ ኣይኣክልን ። ካብ ቦታ ምኣልዮም ። ሕብረተሰብ ። ሓቢርና ።

3Protect religious institutions — demand separation from political agendas

The church must not be a political instrument. Every member of the community has both the right and the responsibility to insist that religious institutions remain places of genuine spiritual care and principled leadership — free from the infiltration of political actors who use religious authority for their own ends. This means standing with righteous leaders when they are threatened, refusing false accusations made for political purposes, and building the community solidarity that protects institutions from capture.

ቤተ ክርስቲያን ካብ ፖለቲካ ። ምምሳጥ ። ቅኑዓት ምሕላው ። ሓቢርና ።

4Take collective responsibility — serve the people from this exploitative system

This is the work of all of us. Not of leaders alone. Not of the politically engaged alone. Of every member of the community who sees what is happening and understands what it means. The exploitative system does not sustain itself alone — it depends on the silence, the division, and the passivity of those it harms. Breaking that dependence through organised, collective, public action is how communities free themselves. We must not be silent. We must serve our people together.

ናይ ኩላትና ዕዮ ። ስቅ ክንብሎ የብልናን ። ካብ ተበላጺ ስርዓት ። ህዝቢ ።

5Unite — recognise those without standing, and agree together to return wealth to the people

The first and most essential act is unity. Among us there are those who aspire to rule the people while having no qualification whatsoever — no record, no legitimate standing — yet who exploit the people's trust and goodness to place themselves above the community. These are people of pure personal interest. We must agree on this truth: a person is honoured for their genuine profession and capacity. Those who enter where they do not belong, who make noise from a position they have no right to hold, must be recognised and removed. And we must agree together that the people's wealth must return to the people — that our people must find peace, live in peace in their homeland according to their own will and ability. It is by agreement and unity that we defeat the enemy of our people — together.

ክንሓብር ። ቦታ ዘይብሉ ። ናይ ህዝቢ ሃብቲ ናብ ህዝቢ ። ሓቢርና ።

6Restore the church to its own leaders — administer its wealth and people through proper servants

The church must be led and served by its own leaders. The people must receive the service they are owed from servants of the church who are accountable to them. The wealth of the church and the welfare of the congregation are the responsibility of the church's own proper leadership — and must be administered by them alone. This is not a political question; it is a question of integrity. A church administered from outside — by those who have no legitimate place within it — is not a church serving its people. It is an institution that has been captured. The restoration of the church to its own leaders is the restoration of the people's right to be served, protected, and led with integrity.

ቤተ ክርስቲያን ብመራሕታ ። ህዝቢ ኣገልግሎት ። ሃብቲ ናብ መራሕቲ ። ምምላስ ።

7Hold open dialogue three times a year — politicians must account to the people

Regular, structured public meetings between the people and their political leaders must be built into civic life — not as occasional gestures, but as a guaranteed, recurring practice. Three times a year, open dialogue must take place: covering the annual progress of the community, financial administration and how resources are being used, the level of educational development of our people, and every other matter that affects their lives. These meetings must be people-centred — not performances for politicians, but genuine accountability sessions in which the people hear, question, and hold their leaders to account. A people without regular access to open dialogue with those who claim to lead them is a people without the most basic protection against exploitation and misuse of their collective wealth.

ኣኼባ ሰለስተ ጊዜ ። ፖለቲከኛታት ። ግሉጽ ዘተ ። ህዝቢ ።

እስከ ሕብረት ብዘይ ኣፈላላይ ንፈትኖ — ዘለዎ መቀረት ሽዑ ክርደኣና እዩ ። ብቅልጡፍ ለዉጢ ናይ ዕብየት ከነርኢ ኤና ። ሽዑ እዩ እታ ጥቅሚ ናይ ሕብራትን ምትሕግጋዝን ዝርደኣና ።

Let us try unity without discrimination — then we will understand its taste. We will quickly show the change of growth. Then we will understand the true benefit of unity and mutual support.
